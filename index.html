Why This Matters

Addressing student debt at a societal level starts with awareness—understanding exactly what you’re committing to before signing. Too often, young adults make major financial decisions tied to FAFSA and student loans without fully grasping how long those obligations will follow them. These choices can shape everything from career flexibility to housing and savings for years, even decades. Using a student debt calculator can help estimate how long repayment will take and what the true cost of borrowing will be over time. Taking a few minutes to run those numbers now can prevent years of unexpected financial strain later.
